Virtual Wind Turbine Lab

Welcome to the Virtual Wind Turbine Lab! In this simulation, you will explore how different factors affect the power output of a wind turbine. By changing variables like blade number, blade length, wind speed, and blade angle, you'll gather data and draw conclusions about optimal wind turbine design.

Instructions:

  1. Adjust the turbine design parameters (number of blades, blade length, blade angle)
  2. Set the wind speed
  3. Click "Run Test" to see how your turbine performs
  4. Record your data in the data table
  5. Try different combinations to find the optimal design

Lab Tasks

  1. Investigation 1: Effect of Number of Blades

    Test how the number of blades affects power output while keeping other variables constant. Record at least 5 tests.

  2. Investigation 2: Effect of Blade Length

    Test how blade length affects power output while keeping other variables constant. Record at least 5 tests.

  3. Investigation 3: Effect of Wind Speed

    Test how wind speed affects power output while keeping other variables constant. Record at least 5 tests.

  4. Investigation 4: Finding the Optimal Design

    Based on your data, design what you think will be the most efficient turbine configuration. Test your design and record the results.

  5. Lab Report

    Create a lab report that includes:

    • Your hypothesis for each investigation
    • Your data tables
    • Graphs of your results
    • Analysis of the optimal turbine design
    • Conclusion: What did you learn about wind turbine design? How does this relate to real-world wind farms?
